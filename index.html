The house of Amnaya

It began with
a mother’s hands.

A child’s fascination with fabric. A woman’s longing to find her own voice. Amnaya grew from these deeply personal beginnings.

Founded by Yumilah Govinden and Jeevesh Augnoo, ours is a conscious fashion house where personal identity, craftsmanship and heritage take form.

We believe the most meaningful luxury is felt: in a piece that belongs to you, a memory it awakens, a story it carries forward.

Meet the way we make
Yumilah & JeeveshFOUNDERS, AMNAYA

The luxury of
human hands.

Behind every piece is knowledge worth preserving. A conversation between the hands that make it and the woman who gives it life.

The materials we choose

Care begins with what we touch. Our approach places thoughtfulness at the heart of each choice, from the feel of a fabric to the way a piece lives with you.

The knowledge we carry

Craftsmanship carries memory. We see making as a way to keep knowledge alive and give expression to the heritage we recognise within ourselves.

The stories we pass on

A treasured piece gathers meaning over time. We imagine clothes as chapters in a woman’s life, holding something of who she is and what she hopes to leave behind.

Good fit begins
with you.

Use your usual size as a starting point. Final measurements and fit are discussed with the atelier before an order is agreed.

Bust

Measure around the fullest part, keeping the tape level and comfortably loose.

Waist

Measure around your natural waist, without drawing the tape tight.

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips with your feet together.

The collection is a creative preview. S, M, L and XL indicate preferences for your enquiry, rather than a confirmed garment size chart.
